# Pathological personal history — visual atlas coverage

This file tracks only clinically justified visual resources. Visible findings may use verified clinical photographs; internal diseases without a diagnostic external appearance use educational anatomy/schematic resources.

## Already integrated
- Varicella — CDC PHIL public-domain clinical photograph.
- Smallpox — CDC PHIL public-domain historical clinical photograph.
- Measles — CDC PHIL public-domain clinical photograph.
- Rubella — CDC PHIL public-domain clinical photograph.
- Atopic dermatitis — Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Psoriasis — Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0.

## Pending offline resources
- Gastrointestinal/hepatic: anatomy plus relevant visible/oral findings.
- Rheumatologic/autoimmune: observable skin, joint and mucosal findings.
- Cardiovascular: anatomy/schematic; no diagnosis by facial appearance.
- Endocrine/metabolic: anatomy plus verifiable clinical findings; no body stereotypes.
- Respiratory: anatomy/studies plus relevant oral effects.
- Renal/urinary: kidney anatomy plus real clinical signs where appropriate.
- Hematologic: pallor, petechiae/ecchymoses and oral findings where documented.
- Neurologic: anatomy/functional schematics; no diagnosis by appearance.

## Acceptance criteria
Every visual must have a source and rights statement, be packaged for offline use when licensing permits, include dental relevance, and avoid implying that a photograph alone establishes a systemic diagnosis. Final completion requires a successful Android workflow and APK Preview artifact.
